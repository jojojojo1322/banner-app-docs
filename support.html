Support

Email bobob.dev@gmail.com. We answer within 72 hours, Monday to Friday (Korea time, UTC+9).

To help us help you, please include:

  • Your Confluence site address (for example your-company.atlassian.net).
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot if something looks wrong.

Please don't send passwords, API tokens or confidential page content.

Feature requests

Tell us what you need and why — we read every request and review them every quarter.

Security issues

Report vulnerabilities to bobob.dev@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject. See Security.

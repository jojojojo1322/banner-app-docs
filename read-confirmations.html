Read confirmations

Ask people to confirm they have read something, see who has, and keep a record for audits.

One confirmation for a banner

Turn on Ask readers to confirm with a "Got it" button. The banner stays until each person clicks Got it. Open the banner in the app to see:

  • Confirmed: who confirmed and when.
  • Not confirmed yet: if the banner targets one or more groups, click Find who hasn't confirmed for the list and the confirmation rate.

One confirmation per page (policies and SOPs)

  1. Start from the Read & confirm template. It asks for a confirmation on every page labelled policy.
  2. Add the policy label to each required page (or choose your own label, or a space).
  3. Optionally target a group, such as all employees, so you can see who hasn't confirmed.
  4. Publish.

Each page now asks for its own confirmation — one label covers 20 or 200 pages, with no per-page setup. In the app you see each page with its number of confirmations; pick a page to see who confirmed it and who hasn't.

A page nobody has confirmed yet is listed too, with everyone in the targeted groups as "Not confirmed" — the app finds those pages from the pages you named and from the label, so a new policy shows up before its first confirmation. It lists up to 100 such pages and tells you when there are more. Such pages are only listed if you can open them yourself, so a restricted page is left out.

When a page changes

Turn on Ask again when the page is edited (on by default in the Read & confirm template). Each confirmation records the page version it was given for. When someone edits the page, everyone who confirmed an older version sees the banner again. The app shows, per page, how many people are on an older version, and they count as not confirmed until they confirm again.

Confirmations given before you turned the option on count until the page is next edited.

Ask again every quarter, half year or year

Set Ask again every to Quarter, Half year or Year. At the start of each period, earlier confirmations stop counting and people see the banner again until they confirm for the new period. Periods start at midnight UTC on 1 January, April, July and October (quarter), 1 January and July (half year), or 1 January (year).

A repeating banner already asks again each round, so the option is off for it. A Confirm by date from an earlier period doesn't make the banner urgent in the new one.

My confirmations

Everyone can open My confirmations from Confluence's Apps menu, or from the All my confirmations link on a banner that asks for confirmation. It lists the banners they still have to confirm, soonest deadline first, with overdue and due-soon ones marked.

  • A banner not limited to particular pages, spaces or labels can be confirmed right in the list.
  • Otherwise the list links to the banner's pages or spaces — confirm it there.
  • A per-page banner lists the pages still waiting, only pages the person can open.

Visitors who aren't signed in have no list and are asked to sign in.

Every confirmation at a glance

Open 📋 Read confirmations in the app's sidebar for all banners that ask for confirmation: confirmed, on an older version, not yet, and the confirmation rate, plus the people who owe the most confirmations. Export all (CSV) puts every banner in one file.

Deadlines

Set Confirm by. The banner shows the date ("Due Oct 16"). In the last 48 hours, people who haven't confirmed also see a yellow line such as "Due in 5 hours" or "Due tomorrow". Once the deadline passes, the banner turns urgent — red, first in line — for everyone who still hasn't confirmed.

Export for audits

Click Export CSV. The file lists each confirmation with name, Atlassian account ID, time (UTC) and, for per-page banners, the page — and, when pages ask again after edits, the version each person confirmed and the page's current version. If the banner targets groups, people who haven't confirmed are included with the status "Not confirmed" — for per-page banners on every page the banner is aimed at, including pages nobody has confirmed yet. A file holds up to 20,000 "Not confirmed" lines; if there are more, it stops there and the app says so. If your browser blocks the download, the same text is shown to copy.

Repeating banners

For a banner that repeats weekly, a confirmation counts for the current round only, so next week's notice asks again and the numbers show this round.

Confirmations are stored in Forge storage in your Atlassian cloud. Deleting a banner deletes its confirmations.

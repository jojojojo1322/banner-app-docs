Announcement Banners & Read Confirmations for Confluence

Schedule, target and track announcements in Confluence — no Premium, no HTML.

Put a friendly banner at the top of Confluence for the people who need it, when they need it — and know who has read it.

What you can do

  • Schedule without Premium. Start and end dates, weekly repeats for maintenance windows and meetings, and several banners at once.
  • Reach the right people. Target spaces, groups and page labels. Show banners to members, to everyone including guests and anonymous visitors, or to visitors only.
  • Let space admins help. Space admins can post banners for their own space. Confluence admins can turn this off.
  • Know who read it. Add a "Got it" button, see who confirmed and who hasn't, set a confirm-by deadline and export a CSV. For policies, ask for a confirmation on every page.
  • Make it look right. 12 designs from a quiet one-line notice to a big spotlight, in light and dark themes.
  • Speak their language. Add translations; each person sees the one for their Confluence language.
  • Move from Data Center in a minute. Paste the HTML of your Announcement Banner for Confluence (Data Center) and it becomes a banner.

Built on Forge

The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. Banners and confirmations are stored in Forge storage in your Atlassian cloud; nothing is sent to servers of ours or anyone else's. See Privacy and Security.
